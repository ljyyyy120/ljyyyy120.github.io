About

Building knowledge that lasts.

Working at the intersection of AI, probabilistic decision-making, and infrastructure monitoring.

My research explores how intelligent systems can make reliable decisions in complex, uncertain physical environments. By combining probabilistic decision-making, POMDPs, information theory, and reinforcement learning, I develop methods that enable infrastructure systems to operate more safely, efficiently, and autonomously.
