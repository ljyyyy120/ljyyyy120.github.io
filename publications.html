NOI-POMDP: A Noise-Explicit POMDP Framework for Structural Health Monitoring

Junyi Li, Eleonora Maria Tronci

Proceedings of the 12th European Workshop on Structural Health Monitoring (EWSHM 2026)
Toulouse, France • July 7–10, 2026. We propose NOI-POMDP, a noise-aware Partially Observable Markov Decision Process framework that explicitly models time-varying sensor noise for Structural Health Monitoring. By integrating the noise-generating process into belief updates, the framework improves state estimation, policy robustness, and long-term maintenance decisions under uncertain sensing conditions.
