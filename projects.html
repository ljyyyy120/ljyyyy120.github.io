2025

GPU-Accelerated Urban Flood Simulation Framework

Developed a high-resolution urban pluvial flood simulation framework that integrates spatial rainfall interpolation, hydrological runoff modeling, sewer capacity constraints, and GPU-accelerated weighted cellular automata. The platform enables rapid flood forecasting and scenario analysis for green infrastructure planning and urban resilience.

Identifying Burglary Risks with Google Street View Images

Developed machine learning models to predict burglary risk in New York City by integrating Google Street View imagery, historical crime records, and demographic data.
